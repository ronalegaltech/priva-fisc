# PRIVA-FISC

Proofs over signed fiscal records.

In Montenegro's electronic fiscalization system (EFI, law 46/2019) every invoice leaves two signed
files with the taxpayer: the request the taxpayer signed and sent to the tax authority, and the
receipt the authority signed back. PRIVA-FISC takes a run of such pairs and produces one proof of a
statement like "these are 10 consecutive invoices of one cash register, each with a valid receipt,
and their total is at least 5,000 EUR", without showing the invoices to whoever checks the proof.

Status: research prototype. It has not been reviewed by anyone outside the team. Do not rely on it.

## What a proof says

For one taxpayer key and one authority key:

- every invoice request is signed by the taxpayer key;
- every receipt is signed by the authority key and names the request it answers;
- all invoices come from the same business unit and device, in the stated year;
- the ordinals are consecutive, so nothing inside the run was left out;
- no receipt is used twice;
- the sum of the totals is at least the stated threshold.

The verifier sees the two key hashes, the year, the threshold, how many invoices there are, whether
the run starts at ordinal 1, and per-invoice nullifiers and opening commitments. It does not see
amounts, parties, invoice codes or ordinals.

## What a proof does not say

- That the authority saw the content of an invoice. The authority's signature covers only the
  request identifier and the receipt code. A taxpayer who registers cheap invoices and then signs
  different content under the same identifiers would pass. The answer is a spot check: the verifier
  picks a few invoices at random, the taxpayer opens those, and the verifier compares them with the
  authority's public invoice lookup. See `tools/efi/opening.py`.
- That no invoices exist after the last one, or on another device of the same taxpayer.
- That an invoice was paid or corresponds to a real sale.

## Layout

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `noir/` | Circuits. `pf_xmldsig` (XML signature checks), `pf_efi` (EFI request, receipt, batch and aggregation logic), `batch_k1`, `batch_k10` (1 and 10 invoices in one proof), `agg_*` and `pf_agg` (two batches of 10 joined into one proof). See `noir/README.md`. |

| `registry/` | Authority keys a verifier may accept. |
| `docs/SDD-v0.5.md` | Design document (in Turkish). |

## Running it

You need Noir 1.0.0, Barretenberg 6.0.0, Scarb 2.21.0, Starknet Foundry 0.64.1 with
universal-sierra-compiler, Garaga built from commit `d97b4d3`, starknet-devnet 0.10.1, and Python
3.12 with `lxml` and `cryptography`. `tools/toolchain.env` expects them under `~/.pf-toolchain` and
puts them on the path.

```sh
. tools/toolchain.env

python3 tools/pf.py prove --synthetic 10 --out /tmp/proof    # 10 invented invoices
python3 tools/pf.py verify /tmp/proof

python3 tools/efi/test_batch.py --k 10        # attacks the 10-invoice circuit must refuse
python3 tools/efi/test_agg.py                 # same for the aggregation, through proving
(cd starknet/pf_registry && snforge test)     # contract tests against a real proof
sh tools/starknet/devnet_demo.sh              # declare, deploy, submit, replay on a local network
```

With real files, give the request and receipt of each invoice in ordinal order:

```sh
python3 tools/pf.py prove --out DIR --threshold 5000.00 --year 2026 req1.xml resp1.xml req2.xml resp2.xml ...
```

`verify` does not trust a verification key shipped with a proof. It derives the key from the
circuit in this repository and looks the authority key up in `registry/issuer_keys.json`.

## Measurements

Apple M3, 16 GB.

| | Gates | Proving | Memory | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 invoice | 417,256 | 5 s | 0.9 GB | 9.9 KB |
| 10 invoices | 4,086,980 | 23 s | 6.6 GB | 11.1 KB |
| 20 invoices (2 x 10, joined) | 4,088,059 per leaf, 1,470,543 root | 99 s in total | 4.0 GB | 10.7 KB |

On a local Starknet network the 10-invoice proof is accepted in one transaction of about 864M L2
gas. It has not been deployed to a public network.

## Limits

- The circuits read requests of up to 2,048 canonical bytes whose `<Invoice>` start tag fits in
  1,280 bytes, with timestamps that carry a numeric offset. Anything else cannot be proved (it does
  not produce a wrong proof).
- Seller tax number, invoice type and corrective references are not read yet.
- One root holds two batches today; larger sets need a deeper tree.
- The only real authority key in the registry is the 2020 test-environment signer.

## Data

Everything in this repository is synthetic: invented invoices signed by throw-away keys. The one
exception is the public certificate data of the EFI test signer in `registry/`. Real invoices,
taxpayer certificates and private keys do not belong here.
